## Asset Requirements

### Favicon
- **favicon.ico** - 32x32px or 16x16px
- Generate at: https://favicon.io/

### Open Graph Image
- **og-image.png** - 1200x630px
- Used for social media previews
- Should include your name and title

### Skill Icons
- Size: 48x48px to 128x128px
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Transparent background preferred

### Project Images
- Size: 800x600px or similar aspect ratio
- Format: PNG, JPG, or WebP
- High quality screenshots

## Where to Find Icons

1. **Icons8** - https://icons8.com/
2. **Flaticon** - https://www.flaticon.com/
3. **Iconfinder** - https://www.iconfinder.com/
4. **DevIcon** - https://devicon.dev/
5. **Simple Icons** - https://simpleicons.org/

## Quick Start

If you don't have custom assets yet, the portfolio will work with:
- Icon URLs from img.icons8.com (already configured)
- Placeholder images (initials shown)
- Default favicon

Replace these with your own assets as you collect them!
